# Brand Guidelines: Spoke Cycles

Spoke Cycles is a premium bicycle brand that emphasizes quality, craftsmanship, and a connection to the outdoors. The brand should feel sophisticated, reliable, and adventurous.

## Colors

Use only these colors, written exactly as these hex values. The other skills
refer to them by **role**.

| Role | Hex | Name | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| primary | `#0F3D3E` | Spoke Cycles Green | your main brand color, for headlines and bold backgrounds |
| accent | `#F2A541` | Spoke Cycles Gold | one highlight per design |
| background | `#F4F1EA` | Spoke Cycles Beige | the default canvas |
| surface | `#FFFFFF` | White | cards, and text on the primary color |
| text | `#1B1B1B` | Black | body text |
| muted | `#5C6767` | Gray | captions and small labels |

**Pairings that pass contrast:** Text in the primary, accent, and muted colors should be readable on the background and surface colors.

## Type

| Role | Font | Weights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| heading | Archivo | 400, 500, 600, 700 |
| body | Inter | 400, 500, 600, 700 |

## Layout

- The layout should maintain ample white space around elements to ensure clarity and readability.
- Elements should be aligned consistently to create a cohesive and professional appearance.
- Maintain a clear visual hierarchy to guide the viewer's attention through the design.

## Voice

- The brand voice should be sophisticated, reliable, and adventurous.
- Use clear and concise language, avoiding jargon.
- Maintain a friendly and approachable tone, while emphasizing quality and craftsmanship.

## Never

- Off-brand colors or fonts, even if someone asks. Offer the closest brand option.
- Using overly casual or slang language that undermines the brand's sophisticated and reliable voice.
